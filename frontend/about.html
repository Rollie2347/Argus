◉ Argus

A hundred eyes, so you don't have to.

Argus is a real-time AI companion for iOS that sees your world through your camera, hears you naturally, and helps with everyday things — cooking, shopping, troubleshooting, and remembering what matters to you — powered by Google's Gemini Live API.

What Argus does

  • Real-time vision — understands what's in front of your camera as you talk
  • Natural voice — talk naturally, interrupt freely, no wake words
  • Persistent memory — remembers your preferences and allergies across sessions
  • Weather-aware — gives locally relevant context for your day
  • Kitchen & shopping help — recipe suggestions, timers, label reading, shopping lists
  • Fix-it help — diagnoses visible problems and talks you through repairs
