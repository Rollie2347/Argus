Argus Audio Diagnostic

Measures whether iOS reduces the audio hardware sample rate when the microphone becomes active. Argus must hold the mic and the speaker at the same time (.playAndRecord); Safari playing a file normally does not. If the rate drops when the mic turns on, every frequency above half that rate is discarded — which is what "muffled" means.
